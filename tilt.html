Tilt Checklist

If any one of these appears, stop trading for five minutes.
Increasing size to recover
Trying to make back losses by sizing up.
Entering faster than normal
Clicking before the setup is fully formed.
Re-entering the same ticker repeatedly
Chasing the same idea after it has already failed.
Feeling angry at a stop-out
Trading to prove the market wrong.
Thinking about daily P&L instead of the setup
Focusing on getting back to green rather than trading quality.
Saying “just one more”
The urge for one final trade is itself a warning sign.
Holding a failed momentum trade
Refusing to realise the loss because you want it to come back.
Any one signal = 5-minute stop.
Step away from the screen. No orders, no re-entry, no size increase.
WASPN rule:

The thought “I can get this back” is a stop signal, not a trading signal.
