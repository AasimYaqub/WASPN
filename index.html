How to Use WASPN Each Trading Day

Use the tools in this order. Not every page needs to be used after every trade.
1
Before trading — Live Risk Dashboard
Open this before the session. Start at Attempt 0 and $0 realised P&L. Keep it as the main live control screen.
2
When entering a trade — Trade Duration Warning
Start the timer when the position is entered. Reassess at 3, 5 and 10 minutes. The purpose is to stop a failed momentum trade quietly becoming a hope trade.
3
After the trade — Trading Journal
Record the completed attempt: ticker, entry time, exit time, entry price, exit price, shares, fees, setup, reason for entry, reason for exit, lesson and whether tilt was present. Repeat for a maximum of five attempts.
4
After a losing trade — Post-Trade Reset
Complete this before taking another entry. If the next trade is really an attempt to recover the previous loss, take the five-minute stop.
5
If mentality changes — Tilt Checklist
Open this whenever anger, urgency, repeated re-entry, increased size, fixation on daily P&L, or “just one more” appears. Any active tilt signal means a five-minute stop.
6
Session finished — Save the Journal Day
Complete the daily notes and save the day. The journal stores Trades 1–5 and the calculated daily P&L. The saved result is then read automatically by the 2026 calendar on this device.
7
End of trading — Daily Compliance
Answer three questions: Did I stay inside the trading window? Did I stop by attempt 5? Did I respect the -$100 hard stop?
8
Final review — Session Score
Score the session out of five based on process. A controlled red day can be a better WASPN day than a green day produced by broken rules.
9
Review progress — 2026 Interactive Calendar
Use the calendar to review daily and monthly performance. Journal-saved dates are marked with a J.
SESSION ENDS: Attempt 5, realised P&L at -$100, or the end of the allowed trading window. No reopening later.

Why the Structure Changed

The 2026 data shows that time-of-day and attempt count matter together. Trading from 7–9 AM ET was materially negative, while the first five attempts inside the 9–11 AM ET window were positive in the sample.

The main problem has not been a lack of winning trades. It has been allowing a small number of losing sessions to expand into catastrophic red days.

October Lesson

Three major red days — 5 October, 15 October and 22 October — accounted for approximately -$2,017.15 combined.

The other October sessions through 23 October combined were approximately +$569.16 .

WASPN therefore does not need bigger home runs. It needs fewer catastrophic continuation sessions.
