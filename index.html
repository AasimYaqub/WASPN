Live Trading Workflow

Follow this order for every trade. You do not need to use every page every time.
BEFORE ENTRY
1
Keep Live Risk open
Check that you are inside an allowed WASPN window and that Attempt count, realised P&L and Tilt state are correct.
2
Decide whether the setup is valid
Do not increase the attempt count yet. A trade becomes an attempt only after it has been completed.
WHEN YOU ENTER THE TRADE
3
Start Trade Duration immediately
Start the timer when the position is entered. The timer is for reassessment while the position is still open.
4
Manage the trade
Trade Duration can prompt reassessment at 3, 5 and 10 minutes. Do not fill out Journal while you are still managing the position.
AFTER THE TRADE CLOSES
5
Update Live Risk
Increase the Attempt count by one and update realised P&L to the new session total.
6
Record the completed trade in Journal
Enter the ticker, times, setup and result. Use Actual / Broker Net P&L when available.
7
Decide whether you are allowed to continue
If the session has hit Attempt 5, -$100 realised or 11:00 AM ET, you are finished.
THE NORMAL LOOP
Live Risk open → Enter trade → Start Trade Duration → Exit trade → Update Live Risk → Record in Journal → Repeat only if WASPN still allows it
USE THESE ONLY WHEN NEEDED
Post-Trade Reset
Use after a meaningful losing trade and before considering another entry.
Tilt Checklist
Use when you notice urgency, anger, repeated re-entry, recovery thinking or “just one more”.
ATTEMPT 5 • -$100 REALISED • 11:00 AM ET
ANY ONE = SESSION OVER
NO REOPENING LATER

End-of-Session Workflow

WHEN TRADING IS FINISHED
1
Save Journal
Confirm all completed attempts and the final daily P&L are correct.
2
Complete Daily Compliance
Answer the three structural questions: window, Attempt 5 and -$100 hard stop.
3
Complete Session Score
Compliance is imported automatically. Answer the two remaining behavioural questions.
4
Check Calendar
Confirm P&L, trade count, compliance and Session Score all appear correctly.
