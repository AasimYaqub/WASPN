Why the Structure Changed

The 2026 data shows that time-of-day and attempt count matter together. Trading from 7–9 AM ET was materially negative, while the first five attempts inside the 9–11 AM ET window were positive in the sample.

The main problem has not been a lack of winning trades. It has been allowing a small number of losing sessions to expand into catastrophic red days.

October Lesson

Three major red days — 5 October, 15 October and 22 October — accounted for roughly -$2,015 combined.

The rest of October through 23 October combined was approximately +$574.

WASPN therefore does not need bigger home runs. It needs fewer catastrophic continuation sessions.
