How to Use WASPN Each Trading Day

1
Open Live Risk
Keep attempts and realised P&L current. The page autosaves.
2
Start Trade Duration on entry
Reassess at 3, 5 and 10 minutes.
3
Record the completed attempt in Journal
Use the broker's actual net P&L when available.
4
After a meaningful loss, use Post-Trade Reset
Do this before deciding on another entry.
5
Use Tilt Checklist when triggered
Any active tilt signal = five-minute stop.
6
Save Journal at session end
This updates daily P&L and the calendar.
7
Save Daily Compliance
Window, Attempt 5 and -$100 are answered once here.
8
Complete Session Score
The first three answers are imported from Daily Compliance automatically.
9
Review Calendar
Review process and P&L together, not one trade in isolation.
